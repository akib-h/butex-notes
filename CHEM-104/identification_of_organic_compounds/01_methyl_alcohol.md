# Methyl Alcohol (CH₃OH) — Identification

## Physical Properties

* Colourless, mobile liquid with a sharp odour.
* B.P. = 64.5–64.7 °C.
* Specific gravity ≈ 0.79 (at 0 °C).
* Soluble in water in all proportions.
* Good solvent for oils and fats.
* Toxic — metabolised in the body to formaldehyde and formic acid; can cause blindness or death if ingested.

## Tests

### (i) Formaldehyde Test

2–3 ml MeOH in a test tube + red-hot copper gauze/spiral, dipped repeatedly
→ smell of formaldehyde (HCHO) + H₂O

**Observation:** Sharp, pungent smell of formaldehyde.

**Reaction:**

$$\ce{CH3OH + [O] ->[Cu, \Delta] HCHO + H2O}$$

### (ii) Methyl Salicylate Test

2 ml MeOH + a little salicylic acid + conc. H₂SO₄, warm gently
→ methyl salicylate + H₂O

**Observation:** Sweet, wintergreen-like smell.

**Reaction:**

$$\ce{CH3OH + C6H4(OH)COOH ->[conc. H2SO4][\Delta] C6H4(OH)COOCH3 + H2O}$$

### (iii) Iodoform Test

MeOH + I₂ solution + NaOH, warm
→ **no** yellow precipitate

**Observation:** No iodoform forms — negative test.

**Inference:** Rules out a CH₃CH(OH)– or CH₃CO– grouping; confirms methanol has only one carbon, so no such fragment can form.

### (iv) Formation of Acetate (Esterification)

MeOH + anhydrous CH₃COOH + conc. H₂SO₄, warm
→ methyl acetate + H₂O

**Observation:** Faint fruity smell (weaker than ethyl acetate).

**Reaction:**

$$\ce{CH3OH + CH3COOH ->[conc. H2SO4][\Delta] CH3COOCH3 + H2O}$$

## Uses of Methyl Alcohol

1. Industrial production of formaldehyde (HCHO).
2. Laboratory solvent.
3. Antifreeze in engine coolant systems.
4. Used to produce methylated (denatured) spirit.

## Comparison Note

See [02_ethyl_alcohol.md](02_ethyl_alcohol.md) — methyl and ethyl alcohol are always compared side by side in the practical viva; the iodoform test is the single fastest way to tell them apart (negative for MeOH, positive for EtOH).
