# Ethyl Alcohol (C₂H₅OH) — Identification

## Physical Properties

* Colourless, volatile liquid with a sweet smell.
* B.P. = 78.3 °C.
* Specific gravity ≈ 0.789.
* Soluble in water in all proportions.
* Soluble in ether, chloroform, etc.
* Good solvent for organic compounds.

## Tests

### (i) Iodoform Test

EtOH + I₂ solution + NaOH, warm
→ **yellow precipitate** of iodoform

**Observation:** Yellow crystalline precipitate with an antiseptic (iodoform) smell.

**Reaction:**

$$\ce{C2H5OH + 4I2 + 6NaOH -> CHI3 v + HCOONa + 5NaI + 5H2O}$$

### (ii) Acetaldehyde Test (Oxidation)

EtOH + K₂Cr₂O₇ + conc./dil. H₂SO₄, warm
→ green colour solution + smell of acetaldehyde

**Observation:** Orange dichromate colour turns green; sharp smell of acetaldehyde.

**Reaction:**

$$\ce{CH3CH2OH + [O] -> CH3CHO + H2O}$$

(Cr₂O₇²⁻ is reduced from orange Cr(VI) to green Cr(III).)

### (iii) Ethyl Acetate Test (Esterification)

EtOH + anhydrous CH₃COOH + conc. H₂SO₄, warm
→ ethyl acetate + H₂O

**Observation:** Strong, pleasant fruity (pineapple-like) smell.

**Reaction:**

$$\ce{CH3COOH + C2H5OH ->[conc. H2SO4][\Delta] CH3COOC2H5 + H2O}$$

### (iv) Ethyl Salicylate Formation

EtOH + salicylic acid + conc. H₂SO₄, warm
→ ethyl salicylate + H₂O

**Observation:** Fruity/wintergreen-type smell.

## Observation Table: Methyl Alcohol vs Ethyl Alcohol

| Reagent | Methyl alcohol | Ethyl alcohol |
|---|---|---|
| Iodoform test | No reaction | Yellow ppt. of iodoform |
| K₂Cr₂O₇ + H₂SO₄ | Smell of HCHO | Green solution, smell of acetaldehyde |
| Anhydrous CH₃COOH + conc. H₂SO₄ | Faint/no smell (methyl acetate) | Fruity smell of ethyl acetate |
| Salicylic acid + conc. H₂SO₄ | Smell of methyl salicylate | Smell of ethyl salicylate |

## Comparison Note

See [01_methyl_alcohol.md](01_methyl_alcohol.md). Iodoform test is the fastest distinguishing test: methanol negative, ethanol positive, because ethanol has the required CH₃CH(OH)– grouping and methanol does not.
