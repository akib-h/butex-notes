# Isopropyl Alcohol / Propan-2-ol ((CH₃)₂CHOH) — Identification

## Physical Properties

* Colourless liquid with a sharp odour (typical "rubbing alcohol" smell).
* B.P. = 82.3 °C.
* Miscible with water in all proportions.
* Secondary alcohol.

## Tests

### (i) Iodoform Test

Sample + I₂ solution + NaOH, warm gently
→ **yellow precipitate** of iodoform

**Observation:** Yellow crystalline ppt., antiseptic smell.

**Reaction:**

$$\ce{(CH3)2CHOH + 4I2 + 6NaOH -> CHI3 v + CH3COONa + 5NaI + 5H2O}$$

**Inference:** Positive iodoform confirms a CH₃CH(OH)– grouping (a "methyl carbinol").

### (ii) Oxidation Test

Sample + K₂Cr₂O₇ + dil. H₂SO₄, warm
→ green colour solution + ketone-like smell (not aldehyde-like)

**Observation:** Orange dichromate turns green; smell is of acetone, not acetaldehyde.

**Reaction:**

$$\ce{(CH3)2CHOH + [O] -> (CH3)2CO + H2O}$$

**Inference:** A secondary alcohol oxidises to a ketone (acetone), not an aldehyde — this is the key point that separates it from ethanol on smell alone.

### (iii) Lucas Test

Sample + Lucas reagent (anhydrous ZnCl₂ in conc. HCl), shake, let stand
→ turbidity/cloudiness appears in about 5–10 minutes

**Observation:** Oily layer/turbidity develops at room temperature without heating, but not instantly.

**Inference:** Confirms a secondary alcohol — faster than a primary alcohol (no reaction at room temperature) but slower than a tertiary alcohol (instant turbidity).
