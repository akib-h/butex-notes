# Acetaldehyde (CH₃CHO) — Identification

## Physical Properties

* Colourless, volatile liquid with a pungent, fruity odour.
* B.P. = 20.2 °C (boils just above room temperature — handle in a cool, capped tube).

## Tests

### (i) Tollens' Reagent Test

CH₃CHO + Tollens' reagent, warm gently
→ silver mirror

**Observation:** Silver mirror deposits on the tube wall.

**Reaction:**

$$\ce{CH3CHO + 2Ag(NH3)2OH -> 2Ag v + CH3COONH4 + 3NH3 + H2O}$$

### (ii) Fehling's Solution Test

CH₃CHO + Fehling's solution, warm
→ brick-red Cu₂O precipitate

**Observation:** Red precipitate forms.

### (iii) Iodoform Test

CH₃CHO + I₂ + NaOH, warm
→ **yellow precipitate** of iodoform

**Observation:** Yellow crystalline ppt., antiseptic smell.

**Inference:** Confirms the CH₃CO– grouping present in acetaldehyde.

### (iv) 2,4-DNP Test

CH₃CHO + 2,4-DNP reagent
→ orange-yellow precipitate

**Observation:** Confirms a carbonyl group is present.

### (v) Schiff's Reagent Test

CH₃CHO + Schiff's reagent
→ pink colour restored

**Observation:** Confirms an aldehyde.

## Comparison Note

See [13_acetone.md](13_acetone.md) — acetaldehyde is Tollens'/Fehling's-positive (it's an aldehyde), while acetone is negative on both (it's a ketone), even though both give a positive iodoform test.
