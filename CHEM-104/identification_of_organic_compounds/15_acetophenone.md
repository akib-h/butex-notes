# Acetophenone (C₆H₅COCH₃) — Identification

## Physical Properties

* Colourless liquid (or low-melting solid) with a sweet, almond-like odour.
* B.P. = 202 °C.
* Aromatic ketone.

## Tests

### (i) Tollens'/Fehling's Test

C₆H₅COCH₃ + Tollens'/Fehling's reagent, warm
→ **no reaction**

**Inference:** Confirms a ketone, not an aldehyde.

### (ii) Iodoform Test

C₆H₅COCH₃ + I₂ + NaOH, warm
→ **yellow precipitate** of iodoform

**Observation:** Yellow crystalline ppt., antiseptic smell.

**Reaction:**

$$\ce{C6H5COCH3 + 3I2 + 4NaOH -> CHI3 v + C6H5COONa + 3NaI + 3H2O}$$

**Inference:** Confirms the CH₃CO– group attached to the aromatic ring.

### (iii) 2,4-DNP Test

C₆H₅COCH₃ + 2,4-DNP reagent
→ yellow-orange precipitate

**Observation:** Confirms the carbonyl group.

### (iv) Baeyer's Test (Dilute KMnO₄)

C₆H₅COCH₃ + dilute KMnO₄
→ purple colour is **not** decolourised by the carbonyl itself under mild cold conditions

**Inference:** Rules out an alkene as the source of unsaturation; the compound is a saturated aromatic ketone.
