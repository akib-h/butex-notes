# Acetamide (CH₃CONH₂) — Identification

## Physical Properties

* White, deliquescent crystalline solid with a faint mousy smell.
* M.P. = 82 °C.
* Very soluble in water.

## Tests

### (i) NaOH + Heat Test

Acetamide + NaOH solution, warm/boil
→ ammonia gas evolved

**Observation:** Litmus paper (moist, red) turns blue; pungent NH₃ smell.

**Reaction:**

$$\ce{CH3CONH2 + NaOH -> CH3COONa + NH3 ^}$$

### (ii) Hydrolysis Test, then NaHCO₃ on the Product

Hydrolyse acetamide (boil with dilute acid or alkali), then test the isolated/acidified product with NaHCO₃.
→ acetic acid regenerated; effervescence with NaHCO₃

**Observation:** CO₂ effervescence confirms the –COOH of the regenerated acetic acid.

### (iii) Hofmann Bromamide Degradation

Acetamide + Br₂ + NaOH
→ methylamine forms

**Observation:** Fishy odour (methylamine); turns moist red litmus blue.

**Inference:** Confirms the classic amide-to-amine degradation, losing one carbon relative to the parent acid (acetamide, 2 C → methylamine, 1 C).
