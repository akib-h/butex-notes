# 02 — Limits

Limits are the foundation everything else in calculus is built on:
derivatives are limits of difference quotients, integrals are limits of
Riemann sums, and series are limits of partial sums. Get comfortable here
and the rest of the course gets noticeably easier.

## Prerequisites

Everything in [`01-prerequisites/`](../01-prerequisites/) — especially
`functions.md` and `limits-review.md`, which already previews some of this
material informally.

## Suggested order

Read 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 in one sitting (they build directly on each other), then
5 → 6 as a pair (continuity leans on the limit definition, and IVT leans on
continuity). Do `examples.md` last, after all six lessons — it deliberately
mixes techniques so you can't rely on "I just read the section this
matches."
